01 About

From NAND gates to RSA,
then how it all breaks.

I'm a computer scientist who likes understanding systems all the way down, then figuring out where they fail.

I'm finishing my B.S. in three years at Texas A&M, where I'm in the Brown Engineering Honors Program and working toward a Cyber Operations Certificate. My work sits where security, cryptography and machine learning meet: penetration testing industrial IoT, implementing cryptosystems from first principles, and building infrastructure to evaluate AI agents at scale.

Outside of research I teach. I'm a TA for Texas A&M's intro Python and C++ courses, and I've tutored students in physics, math and CS for six years.
