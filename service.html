Service

Light and Salt Association

As a regional mental health intern, I planned, organized, and led a team of over 40 youth in providing homeless support. Through a project called hope bags, we were able to organize and mobilize the local asian american youth in packing and distributing care packages for the homeless. Overall we worked with local organizations NHCCW and Compassion United and impacted over 1500 homeless people in the houston area through an event called "Breakfast in the Park".
